GLAD YOU ASKED

A few things
you might be wondering.

Getting your next meal should be easy.

How do I place my first order?+

Create a customer account or sign in, browse restaurants, add dishes to your cart, and complete checkout with your delivery details.

Can I pick up my order instead?+

Yes. Choose pickup when ordering. Available options depend on the restaurant you select.

How can I track my delivery?+

Open Your orders in the customer portal and select your order to see its status and available delivery tracking.

What are the delivery fees and times?+

Delivery details depend on your restaurant and location. Review the charges and estimated timing shown during checkout before placing your order.

How do I join as a partner?+

Use the restaurant or delivery partner signup above to create your account and complete your profile in the relevant portal.
